[ Genre Futuristic Horror ]

The player has to retriece resouces and evidence to uncover the mystery of the facility they are trapped in. 
The player must avoid hostile entities while solving puzzles and navigating through the environment.
The game will feature a dark and atmospheric setting, with a focus on tension and suspense.
The player will have to return to the lobby/spawn to save their progress and unlock new levels.

---

Player will be trapped in a dark room to fight off against an endless hoarde of monsters.
They must traverse 30 floors to the top of the tower
